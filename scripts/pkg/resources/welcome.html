Shoji

A Hyprland-style tiling window manager for macOS, with two companion apps:

  • Shoji — tiles your app windows, with workspaces, gaps and Lucent themes.
  • Shoji Machines — Linux server VMs (⌥M).
  • Shoji Containers — Docker containers and images (⌥O).

All three are installed in your Applications folder. If they're running, they're quit first; Shoji Machines shuts its VMs down cleanly. Your settings and VMs in ~/.config/shoji are kept.

Needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later, and kitty. Shoji Containers needs Docker Desktop, OrbStack or Colima.
